Difficulty labels describe practice level, not school grade boundaries.

Progress is local to the browser and origin. First-check accuracy, current-run results, best completed runs, drafts and written self-assessments are distinct. Written responses are not automatically graded. Retrying auto questions preserves first-check/best results and written drafts; editing a draft invalidates its prior self-assessment until saved again. A separate, confirmed reset clears exam progress without changing the original genetics checks. Progress export produces JSON; no cross-device syncing or import interface is currently provided.

## Timed mock exams

Two fixed mixed-topic papers select existing items from the revision bank, so students may recognise questions from topic practice. Each paper samples all 8 topics with one auto-marked item and one written response per topic. Core: 16 questions, 32 marks, 55 minutes. Extension: 16 questions, 40 marks, 70 minutes. These durations and difficulty labels are practice settings, not official school exam specifications. Optional 25% or 50% extra time is selected before starting.

The timer uses an absolute deadline stored in localStorage. It continues while the tab is hidden or reloaded. If the browser is closed, saved answers are submitted on the next visit after the deadline; the app does not claim to run while closed. An expired attempt cannot accept more answers through the interface. The intended workflow is one browser tab at a time. This client-only practice app is not a secure assessment platform.

Submitted answers are locked. Automatically marked questions score immediately. Blank/invalid answers score zero; nonblank written responses remain pending until self-assessed against the marking points. The combined score and percentage are withheld while written marking is pending. Scores that include written marks explicitly identify their self-assessed basis; no school grade boundaries are inferred. Recommendations use missed auto questions, unanswered items and missing written criteria, with links back to the matching practice topic. A two-question sample per topic is not enough to infer comprehensive mastery.

The current attempt and up to six archived finished attempts persist locally under a separate mock-exam key. Mock attempts do not overwrite topic-practice progress. Result export is JSON. This does not provide automatic cross-device syncing or a teacher dashboard.
